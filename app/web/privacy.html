JobFinder Privacy Policy

Effective 2 October 2026 · JobFinder is operated by Raj Kumar G K (rajkumar.codes) · Contact: support@rajkumar.codes

JobFinder finds job postings that match your resume and prepares application material for you: a tailored resume, a cover letter, a suggestions report and interview practice. This page explains what data that involves, who processes it, and how long it's kept. Anyone can register, and the operator reviews and approves each new account before it can be used.

Information we collect

  • Account: your email address, first and last name, phone number, whether you'd like a mobile app (and for iPhone or Android), and whether your account has been approved. We use these to review your registration and to contact you about your account. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider (Supabase Auth); we never see or store them in readable form.
  • Documents you upload: your resume and cover letter, and the profile extracted from them (skills, experience, education, contact details as written in your resume).
  • Preferences: target roles, locations, keywords, job board links and other settings.
  • API keys you add for AI or job-search providers. They are used only for your own runs and are never shown back in the app (only their last 4 characters).
  • Job data and generated documents: job postings found for you, match and ATS scores, salary information, tailored resumes, cover letters, suggestion reports, interview practice and your progress (for example which jobs you applied to).
  • Activity logs of the job agents (what ran, when, and any errors), used to operate and troubleshoot the service.

The app contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking.

How we use it

Only to provide JobFinder to you: searching for relevant jobs, scoring them against your resume and generating your application material. We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with employers. You decide whether and where to apply.

Service providers that process your data

  • Supabase stores your account, data and files, and handles sign-in.
  • AI providers (Google Gemini, Groq, OpenRouter) receive your resume text, cover letter and job descriptions to produce scores and documents. They process this content under their own terms; some free plans may use submitted content to improve their services.
  • Job search services (Adzuna, JSearch via RapidAPI, Remotive, Arbeitnow and company job boards you add) receive only search terms such as roles and locations, never your resume.
  • GitHub runs the job agents; Cloudflare hosts the website.

Who can see your data

Your data is private to your account: other JobFinder users cannot see it. The operator can access the database and storage to run and maintain the service.

How long we keep it

  • Jobs you never mark as applied are deleted automatically, with their files, after 30 days.
  • Jobs you applied to, your uploaded documents and settings are kept until you delete them or your account.
  • To delete your account and all associated data, email support@rajkumar.codes from the address you sign in with. We delete it within 30 days.

Security

All traffic uses HTTPS. Access to data is restricted per account by database rules, and stored files are private and served only through short-lived links.

Children

JobFinder is not intended for anyone under 16.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact
